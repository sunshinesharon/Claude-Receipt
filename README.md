Claude Builder Receipt 🧾

**Claude Builders deserve receipts.**
What are you building? Who inspires you?

The Claude Builder Receipt turns those answers into a personalized, shareable receipt, complete with your own Claude-generated builder archetype.
It started as an idea for Claude & Coffee NYC and grew into something for Claude builders everywhere, including designers, marketers, product managers, creators, engineers, and anyone turning an idea into something real.

**How it works**

Press the Claude Bot in the center
Enter your name
Share what you are building
Spotlight the builders who inspire you
Print and save your receipt

The blinking eyes, tactile keyboard, sound effects, and receipt-printing moment turn a simple form into a tiny ritual worth remembering.

**The idea**

We celebrate launches, but rarely keep a record of the people and ideas that helped us build them.
This receipt captures both.

**Built with 💚**

There is no external profile or community data source. The builder enters everything directly.
Conceived, designed, and built enthusiastically by **Sharon Jacob, The Digital Buff**, for a community of curious people who keep thinking, connecting, and building.
